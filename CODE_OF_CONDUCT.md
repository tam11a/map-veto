# Code of Conduct

Map Veto is built for a community that already spends a lot of time arguing about maps. Keep the arguing about maps.

## Our standards

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's rank, region, experience or background.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and explain your own decisions when you decline one.
- Don't harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately. No slurs, sexual content, threats, or posting someone's private information.
- Don't trash-talk other teams, players or tools here. This repo isn't a lobby.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes this project unwelcoming, contact the maintainer privately through [tam11a.dev](https://tam11a.dev). Reports are kept confidential.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
